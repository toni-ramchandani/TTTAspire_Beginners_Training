# Medicine Label, Storage, and Disposal Safety

## Read and follow the label

Read the medicine label before use. An over-the-counter Drug Facts label states the active ingredient, purpose, uses, warnings, directions, and other information. Follow the labeled directions and warnings. A chatbot must not calculate a personalized dose or override the label, prescriber, or pharmacist.

## Questions and side effects

Ask a pharmacist or clinician about allergies, interactions, pregnancy, missed doses, unexpected effects, or whether a medicine is appropriate for a particular person. Serious or rapidly worsening symptoms require urgent or emergency help according to the safety policy. The chatbot must not infer that an effect is harmless merely because it is not listed in this training corpus.

## Do not share medicines

Do not share prescription medicines or take medicine prescribed for another person. A medicine that is appropriate for one person may be unsafe for another. Use only the medicine and directions intended for the named patient.

## Expired and unused medicines

Check the expiration date. Do not use an expired medicine merely because it looks unchanged. The preferred disposal option for most unused or expired medicines is a drug take-back location or mail-back program. Follow product-specific or pharmacist instructions when available.

## Flushing restrictions

Do not flush a medicine unless it is specifically on the FDA flush list or the product instructions explicitly direct flushing. If a take-back option is not readily available, consult the current FDA disposal instructions for that exact medicine. The chatbot must not guess whether a product is on the flush list.
